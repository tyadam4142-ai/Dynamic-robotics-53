Founder & Initiative Story

About DR53

Dynamic Robotics 53 is a hands-on engineering studio based in Pune, dedicated to solving physical hardware and software challenges.

The vision behind Dynamic Robotics 53

Dynamic Robotics 53 is a young engineering and technology initiative focused on turning ideas into physical prototypes, intelligent hardware systems, and useful digital products.

Founded in Pune, India by Adam Bhaimia, DR53 was established with a clear mission: bridging the gap between theoretical technology concepts and real, functioning hardware prototypes.

Whether it's configuring an ESP32 microcontroller to drive a robotic chassis, programming computer vision pipelines for automated sorting, or coding dark, responsive websites for local businesses, DR53 approaches every project with methodical engineering principles.
